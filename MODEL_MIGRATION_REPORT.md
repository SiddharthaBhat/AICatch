## 2. Model Role Evaluation & Promotion Analysis

### Role 1: Object Detection & Tracking
- **Active:** `YOLO11s` (Source: `yolo11s.pt`)
- **Candidates Considered:** `YOLO26n`, `YOLO26s`
- **Measured Metrics:**
  - Indexing Throughput: `13.13 FPS`
  - Latency: `76.19 ms`
  - Peak VRAM: `386.56 MB`
  - Ground Truth Accuracy: `NOT AVAILABLE — NO LABELLED GROUND TRUTH DATASET`
- **Decision:** `RETAIN YOLO11s`. Candidate weights are unlabelled on custom CCTV; promotion guard prevents switching without a superior benchmark on labelled datasets.

### Role 2: Visual Semantics
- **Active:** `CLIP ViT-B/32` (Source: `openai/clip-vit-base-patch32`)
- **Candidates Considered:** `SigLIP2 Base` (`google/siglip2-base-patch16-256`)
- **Measured Metrics:**
  - Indexing Throughput: `26.04 FPS`
  - Latency: `38.40 ms`
  - Peak VRAM: `386.56 MB`
  - Embedding Dimension: `512`
  - Ground Truth Accuracy: `NOT AVAILABLE — NO LABELLED GROUND TRUTH DATASET`
- **Decision:** `RETAIN CLIP ViT-B/32`. Seamless multi-modal text/image compatibility and low VRAM footprint.

### Role 3: Person Re-Identification
- **Active:** `MobileNetV3 Re-ID` (Source: `torchvision.models.mobilenet_v3_small`)
- **Candidates Considered:** `OSNet x0.5`

  - Ground Truth Accuracy: `NOT AVAILABLE — NO LABELLED GROUND TRUTH DATASET`
- **Decision:** `RETAIN MobileNetV3 Re-ID`. Fast, zero additional dependency overhead, normalized L2 vectors.

### Role 4: Face Verification
- **Active:** `FaceNet (InceptionResnetV1)` (Source: `facenet_pytorch`)
